How it works

Designed to stay
out of the way.

Pause & Pass works in the background during an active rental trip. Operators decide the appropriate rider disclosure and safety experience for their market, while Pause & Pass supplies recommended privacy language, event logging and configurable intervention rules.

🛵
Trip starts

Rider opens your app

They scan the vehicle and start the trip exactly as they do today. The plugin activates in the background for that active trip. No change to your existing trip flow.

🎵
Crossing zone

A configured intervention triggers

On-device logic evaluates the mapped zone, movement and location confidence. The plugin then requests your configured response — an audio-focus pause, ducking, a chime or an in-app prompt — within the device and OS capabilities.

✅
Crossing cleared

Crossing cleared. Event logged.

After the crossing, normal audio behaviour resumes and a privacy-minimised event — zone ID, timestamp, app/SDK version and outcome — is logged to your operator dashboard.

Step 01

Add the SDK

Your engineer adds the Pause & Pass SDK to your existing Android or iOS project. One import, one initialisation call with your operator key. Nothing else changes in your app.

Step 02

Hook into trip events

Attach to your existing trip start and end callbacks. The SDK evaluates mapped zones on-device during active trips — you keep full control of your app UI and rider experience.

Step 03

Go live & measure

Eligible active trips on supported app versions are now covered. Safety events flow into your operator dashboard, and you own the record for reporting.

⚖️
Transparent by design — we help with the disclosures
Location processing and automated safety interventions should be described in your privacy notice and rider terms. Pause & Pass supplies recommended privacy language, a minimised event schema and configurable intervention rules, so your legal team can set the right disclosure and consent flow for each market. GDPR requires clear information on processing — legal basis, recipients, retention and any automated processing — and we give you the building blocks to provide it.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask.

Does audio always pause and resume exactly?

No — audio behaviour depends on the OS, device and player. We request a configured intervention (pause, duck, chime or prompt) within platform capabilities and validate behaviour across your supported matrix.

What is the battery impact?

The plugin runs during active trips using on-device zone logic and standard location APIs, tuned to minimise battery use. We share measurements during the pilot.

How accurate is zone detection?

Zone entry uses configurable buffers and location-confidence rules to reduce false triggers near boundaries. Accuracy depends on device GPS and conditions.

Do riders need to be told?

You control disclosure. Location processing and automated interventions should be described in your privacy notice and rider terms; we supply recommended language.

What data is retained, and for how long?

Only minimised events — zone ID, timestamp, app/SDK version and outcome. Retention and controller / processor roles are agreed per operator.

Who owns and maintains the zones?

We maintain a mapped zone database for your markets and support custom zones; you decide which zones are active.
