How it works

Designed to stay
out of the way.

Pause & Pass works in the background during an active rental trip. Operators decide the appropriate rider disclosure and safety experience for their market, while Pause & Pass supplies recommended privacy language, event logging and configurable intervention rules.

🗺️
01

Map risk zones

You define high-risk places: railway crossings, tram tracks, dangerous junctions and low-visibility areas.

🛴
02

Detect the active trip

The plugin runs only during an active rental trip — never outside it.

🔇
03

Trigger the intervention

Near a zone it pauses or lowers audio, plays a chime or shows an in-app prompt — you choose which.

🧾
04

Log the event

An auditable record: time, zone ID, app/SDK version, intervention type and technical outcome.

📊
05

Build the report

See zone visits, intervention frequency, system performance and the zones with the highest exposure.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask.

Does audio always pause and resume exactly?

No — audio behaviour depends on the OS, device and player. We request a configured intervention (pause, duck, chime or prompt) within platform capabilities and validate behaviour across your supported matrix.

What is the battery impact?

The plugin runs during active trips using on-device zone logic and standard location APIs, tuned to minimise battery use. We share measurements during the pilot.

How accurate is zone detection?

Zone entry uses configurable buffers and location-confidence rules to reduce false triggers. Limits apply: GPS accuracy, device settings, battery management and the quality of the zone database can all affect how an intervention works.

Do riders need to be told?

You control disclosure. Location processing and automated interventions should be described in your privacy notice and rider terms; we supply recommended language.

What data is retained, and for how long?

Only minimised events — zone ID, timestamp, app/SDK version and outcome. Retention and controller / processor roles are agreed per operator.

Who owns and maintains the zones?

We maintain a mapped zone database for your markets and support custom zones; you decide which zones are active.

Does Pause & Pass guarantee a lower OCTA premium?

No. Pause & Pass is not an insurance product and does not guarantee lower premiums. It creates preventive controls and measurable safety evidence that may support future claims and insurance-risk discussions.

Is extra hardware needed?

No. Pause & Pass is software that integrates into your existing operator app.

Do riders need to download a separate app?

No. It runs inside your existing operator app.

Does it only work with e-scooters?

No. It is built for connected micromobility platforms, including e-scooters, self-propelled bicycles, e-bikes and e-mopeds.
